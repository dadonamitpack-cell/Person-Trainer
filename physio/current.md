## How we progress it (coach rules, within the physio's targets)
- Add **1 rep per side** when every set felt RIR ≥ 2 and pain ≤ 2/10 during and the next day.
- 1-arm TRX row: 4 → 8 e/s (~4 weeks). Then make it harder by walking feet forward.
- 1-arm DB bench: 5 → 8 e/s, then add 2 kg and drop back to 5.
- Cable SL deadlift: start at a weight you can do 8/8 at RIR 3 with a level pelvis; add 2.5–5 kg per week toward 40 kg.
- Towel chin-up: stay at 3 e/s until the physio says otherwise. Slow lowering (3 s), no kipping, no dropping into the bottom.
- **Stop rule:** sharp pain, clicking with apprehension, or the shoulder feeling "loose" → stop that drill and tell the physio.
