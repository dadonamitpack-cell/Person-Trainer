# Block 1: In-season, weeks 1–4 (starts week of 2026-11-08)

**Aim:** build game stamina and repeat-sprint ability, add the missing lower-body strength and injury-prevention
work, fix pull-ups, recover better, and arrive fresh on match day.

## What I changed vs. your current sheet, and why
| Change | Why (evidence) |
|---|---|
| **Added Nordic hamstring curls** | Cut hamstring injuries by ~50% in footballers (van der Horst 2015; van Dyk 2019 meta-analysis). Low volume works. |
| **Kept Copenhagen adductors**, now weekly year-round | ~41% fewer groin problems in amateur footballers (Harøy 2019, BJSM). |
| **Added single-leg balance training for the ankle** | Balance training cut recurrent ankle sprains by ~35% in people with a previous sprain (Hupperets 2009, BMJ). |
| **One heavy lower-body day, every week** | Strength training improves sprint and jump in footballers (Silva 2015 meta-analysis). In-season, 1 session/week maintains strength (Rønnestad 2011). |
| **Added 1 structured HIIT session** | 4×4 min at 90–95% HRmax raised VO₂max ~11% and match running distance ~20% in footballers (Helgerud 2001). |
| **Hard days hard, easy days easy** | Stack heavy lifting + HIIT on the same day, away from the match, so the other days can recover. |
| **Physio drills twice a week** (towel chin-ups = pull-up work for now) | Physio sheet 2026-11-06. Twice a week gives more practice on the skill while the shoulder heals. |
| **Upper body cut back** to fit everything in | Your goal is performance on the pitch; upper body stays at maintenance-plus. |
| Cut "drains metabolites", "max explosive hip thrust" etc. claims | Not supported; the drills themselves are fine. |

## Weekly layout: Saturday match (MD = match day)
| Day | Code | Session | Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sun | MD+1 | **Gym 1:** recovery flush + upper body + physio | Low–moderate |
| Mon | MD-5 | Team training | Moderate |
| Tue | MD-4 | **Gym 2:** heavy lower body + Nordics/Copenhagen + **HIIT 4×4** | **Hard day** |
| Wed | MD-3 | Team training | Moderate |
| Thu | MD-2 | **Gym 3:** upper body + pull-ups + ankle (short, no fatigue) | Low–moderate |
| Fri | MD-1 | Rest, or 15-min sharpness primer (below) | Very low |
| Sat | MD | **Match** | Max |

**Friday-match weeks:** shift every day one earlier (Gym 2 on Mon, Gym 3 on Wed, primer on Thu) and keep two rules:
heavy legs/HIIT at least **72 h before** the match and at least **48 h after** the last one. Nothing hard within 48 h
of kickoff. If the gap is too short, skip the HIIT. Team training already covers some of that work.

## Rules for every session
- **Effort:** finish sets with **2 reps in reserve (RIR 2)**, RIR 3 on Thursday. No sets to failure in-season.
- **Rest:** 2–3 min on main lifts, 60–90 s on accessories.
- **Physio block first:** whatever the physio currently prescribes (`physio/current.md`) goes right after the warm-up.
- **Progression (double progression):** when you hit the top of the rep range on every set at the target RIR,
  add 2.5–5 % load next time.
- **Week 4 = deload:** same exercises, ~half the sets, no HIIT. Also deload early after a congested week or if
  your wellness score drops (see check-in).
- Log every set in `logs/`.

## Gym 1: Sunday (MD+1). Recovery + upper body + physio · ~60 min
1. **Flush:** 15–20 min easy bike (you can talk in full sentences) → Cat-Cow 1 min, 90/90 hip switches 8/side
2. **Physio block (shoulder)**, from `physio/current.md`:
   - 1-arm TRX row 3×4/side (→ 8/side)
   - 1-arm DB bench press 3×5/side (→ 8/side)
   - Towel chin-up 3×3/side, 3-s lowering. **This is your pull-up work for now**: regular chin-up
     progressions wait until the physio clears them.
3. Lat pulldown, neutral grip: 3×8–12 (moderate, controlled, no stretch-and-bounce at the top)
4. Superset: DB curls 2×10–12 + cable pushdown 2×10–12
8. **Ankle:** single-leg balance 3×30 s/leg (progress: eyes closed → soft pad → catching a ball)
9. Core: Dead bug 2×10/side, Side plank 2×30 s/side

## Gym 2: Tuesday (MD-4). The hard day · ~75 min + HIIT
1. **Warm-up:** 5 min bike, World's Greatest Stretch 5/side, leg swings 10+10, side lunge 6/side, 2× (A-skips, 10 m build-up)
2. **Physio block:** single-leg cable deadlift 3×8/leg, level pelvis, build toward 40 kg (`physio/current.md`)
3. **Power:** countermovement box jump 3×4 (step down) · lateral bound with a 2-s stuck landing 3×4/side (ankle control)
4. **Strength:**
   - A. Trap-bar deadlift (or back squat) **4×4–6**, RIR 2
   - B. Bulgarian split squat 3×6–8/leg
   - C. RDL 3×8 (odd weeks) / Hip thrust 3×8–10 (even weeks)
5. **Injury-prevention:**
   - **Nordic hamstring curl:** wk1 2×4 → wk2 2×5 → wk3 3×5 → wk4 2×4 (use a band for help if needed; first
     week will make you sore, which is why it sits 4 days before the match)
   - **Copenhagen adductor:** wk1 short lever (knee on bench) 2×6/side → progress to long lever 3×8–10
   - Bent-knee calf raise 3×12–15 + Tibialis raise 2×15–20
6. **HIIT: Norwegian 4×4** (on the bike or treadmill/hill if your shins are fine)
   - 4 min at **90–95% HRmax** (hard, can say 2–3 words) / 3 min easy jog or spin; wk1 3 reps, wk2–3 4 reps, wk4 none
   - Best done after lifting (protects strength quality) or later that day, ≥6 h apart if possible.

## Gym 3: Thursday (MD-2). Short, crisp, no fatigue · ~45 min
1. Warm-up: Cat-Cow, 90/90, Squat-to-press 2×8
2. **Physio block (shoulder):** 1-arm TRX row 3×4/side · 1-arm DB bench 3×5/side · Towel chin-up 3×3/side
   (same progression as Sunday; this is the second weekly exposure)
3. *(Pull-up progression B paused until physio clears regular chin-ups)*
4. Landmine press 3×8/side (more shoulder-friendly than strict overhead press after a dislocation)
5. Inverted row (TRX/barbell) 3×8–12. Builds pulling strength for pull-ups.
6. **Ankle:** step-downs 2×8/leg + balance 2×30 s/leg
7. Core: Pallof press 2×12/side, Plank 2×40 s
8. Optional speed primer: 3× countermovement jump (max height, full rest). **No** heavy legs, no Nordics.

## Friday (MD-1). Optional 15-min sharpness primer
Mobility 5 min → 4× 10–20 m accelerations at 90%, full walk-back rest → 2× short reactive change-of-direction drill.
Short, sharp, done. Pre-match "activation" like this is common practice; direct evidence is modest. If you feel flat, rest instead.

## Cool-down after any session (5–8 min, optional)
Pigeon 45 s/side, low lunge with twist 45 s/side, child's pose 1 min. Good for relaxation and range of motion; it
does not speed up muscle recovery much. Sleep and food do that.

## Conditioning targets and testing
- **Retest every 4 weeks:** 5 km time (or 30-15 Intermittent Fitness Test if a team-mate can run it with you),
  strict pull-ups max, trap-bar 5RM estimate, morning resting HR.
- To set HIIT zones: estimate HRmax as the highest HR seen in a match or hard 5k finish (better than 220−age).

## Nutrition: ~85 kg, active footballer
**Energy:** maintenance is roughly **2,900–3,300 kcal** depending on the day (Mifflin-St Jeor ≈ 1,840 kcal × activity
1.6–1.8). Formulas are off by ±10–15%, so we correct it from your weekly average weight.
- Optional slow cut (a few kg): ~300–400 kcal below maintenance **on Sun–Thu only**, never on MD-1 / match day.
  Aim ≤0.5% body weight per week (~0.4 kg/week) so it doesn't cost performance.

**Protein:** **150–170 g/day** (1.8–2.0 g/kg), 4 feedings of 30–45 g. You already have ~78 g from breakfast + shake.
Lunch and dinner need ~40 g each (≈150–180 g cooked chicken/meat/fish, or equivalent).

**Carbs: periodize by day** (UEFA expert group statement, Collins et al. BJSM 2021)
| Day type | Carbs | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| Rest / light days (Thu, Fri if off) | 3–4 g/kg | 250–340 g |
| Team training / Gym 2 days | 4–6 g/kg | 340–510 g |
| **MD-1 and match day** | **6–8 g/kg** | **510–680 g** |
More rice/pasta/potatoes/bread/fruit on the big days; more veg and protein on light days. Fat ~1 g/kg (~80–90 g).

**Match day**
- Pre-match meal 3–4 h before: carb-based, lower fat/fibre (e.g. rice/pasta + chicken + light sauce), 1–3 g/kg carbs.
- **Caffeine** 3 mg/kg (~250 mg, e.g. 2 espressos) ~60 min before. Strong evidence for performance (ISSN 2021).
- Half-time: 30–60 g fast carbs (sports drink, banana, gel).
- After the match: within 1–2 h a meal with ~40 g protein + plenty of carbs. Alcohol after a match noticeably slows
  muscle repair (Parr 2014). If you drink, eat and rehydrate first and keep it moderate.
- Hydration: weigh before/after the match; drink ~1.5 L per kg lost.

**Recovery basics that actually move the needle**
1. **Sleep 8 h+** (athletes need more; sleep extension improved sprint and accuracy, Mah 2011).
2. ~40 g casein before bed on Gym 2 and match nights (e.g. your high-protein yogurt / cottage cheese): moderate evidence
   for overnight muscle repair (Snijders 2015).
3. **Creatine monohydrate 3–5 g/day.** Strong evidence for repeat-sprint and strength; safe.
4. Cold-water immersion is fine after the **match**, but avoid it right after **Gym 2**: it blunts strength/muscle
   gains (Roberts 2015).
